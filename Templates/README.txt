STD-AIO image templates

Place your own cropped PNG images of stable Sorcerer Tower Defense UI elements here.
Supported names: start_match.png, placement_ready.png, upgrade_button.png, skip_wave.png.
Capture your own PNG crops at the same UI scale. The macro does not invent templates or placement coordinates.
AutoPlace coordinates are client-area X/Y values in config.ini under [Placement]. Count defaults to 0.
